# Lucene IDF for per-ticker BM25 indexes

**Date:** 2026-10-09

## Context

Per-ticker BM25 statistics (`2026-10-08-bm25-per-ticker-stats.md`) left a known residual.
rank_bm25's `BM25Okapi` computes IDF as `log((N - n + 0.5) / (n + 0.5))`. That is negative for a
word in over half the chunks, and every negative value is raised to `0.25 × average IDF`. Over
MSFT's 1,399 chunks the floor is 1.15. `revenue`, in 604 chunks, has an IDF of 0.27. 30 words
outscore a word found in fewer chunks. Income-statement chunks fell in scoped BM25 rank, and the
replay lost `msft-rd-intensity-fy2025/revenue` (coverage 34 → 33). The live v1 gate stayed at
47/48 (`95f42d9`), so this is a ranking defect measured offline, not a live regression.

## Decision

Per-ticker indexes (`_ticker_indexes`) use `_LuceneIdfBM25`, a `BM25Okapi` subclass whose
`_calc_idf` is Lucene's `log(1 + (N - n + 0.5) / (n + 0.5))`. That IDF is always positive and
strictly decreasing in `n`. The whole-corpus index for untickered searches is unchanged.

## Why

The two candidates and the rule for choosing between them were fixed before either was measured
(primary metric coverage, then hit@5, and both must be at least the baseline):

| Per-ticker IDF | hit@5 (of 981) | Coverage (of 40) | Parts gained / lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Okapi, epsilon 0.25 (HEAD, re-baselined) | 587 | 33 | — |
| A: Okapi, `epsilon=0` | 620 | 36 | 51 / 18 |
| **B: Lucene IDF** | **630** | **36** | **53 / 10** |

- Both candidates regain `msft-rd-intensity-fy2025`, `nvda-inventory-turnover-fy2026` and
  `five-company-net-margin-ranking-fy2025`. Neither loses a question.
- B wins on hit@5 and loses fewer parts. All 10 parts it loses are still in the fused pool and drop
  at fusion: 9 are MSFT segment-table parts on 3 queries, and 1 is an `nvda-gross-margin-fy26`
  part. A also lost those 9, plus 8 more.
- `epsilon=0` keeps the misordering's cause and only removes its effect. Every word in over half
  the chunks scores 0, which also drops a chunk matching only such words at the zero-score cutoff.
  Lucene's IDF has no floor to tune.
- **Rejected: rank_bm25's `BM25Plus`.** Its `get_scores` adds `idf × delta` to every chunk for
  every query word, including chunks without the word. That is a constant shift per query, so it
  brings none of BM25+'s benefit, and no chunk ever scores 0, so the cutoff never fires.
- **Rejected: rank_bm25's `BM25L`.** Its IDF is fine, but its `get_scores` multiplies by the term
  count a second time, which removes term-frequency saturation. Measuring it would mix two changes.
- **Untickered searches keep Okapi.** The replay holds no untickered query, so a change there would
  be unmeasured. Over 19,292 chunks the floor matters less.

## Files touched

- `src/sec_agent/retrieval/retrieval.py`: `_LuceneIdfBM25`, used by `_ticker_indexes`.
- `tests/retrieval/test_retrieval.py`: IDF falls as a word gets commoner. A chunk matching only a
  word in exactly half the chunks still ranks; under Okapi its IDF was 0 and the cutoff dropped it.
- `tests/manual/verify_bm25_ticker_stats.py`: a real-MSFT check that IDF never rises with document
  frequency. Its oracle now uses the adopted class.
